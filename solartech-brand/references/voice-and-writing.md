# SolarTech Voice, Tone & Writing Rules (Full Detail)

Source: SolarTech Branding Guidelines 2026

## Mission & Vision
SolarTech's mission: deliver energy solutions that work for customers, the planet, and the future — people-first, innovative, trustworthy solar services rooted in excellence and accountability. Vision: a future where clean energy is accessible, affordable, and dependable for all.

## Core Values
- **Passionate** — energy and heart in the work, inspiring innovation and creativity
- **Disciplined** — structure and focus, high standards, consistent excellence
- **Accountable** — full responsibility for actions, continuous improvement
- **Trustworthy** — transparency and integrity, promises met or exceeded
- **Customer Centric** — every decision starts with the customer's needs

## Target Audience (for tone-matching copy)
Homeowners aged 35–60, three personas:
- **The Savings Seeker** — budget-conscious, tired of high utility bills → highlight savings, incentives, financing, long-term value
- **The Sustainability Champion** — eco-minded, often EV owners/tech-forward families → emphasize clean energy benefits, solar+EV synergy, real sustainability data
- **The Self Sufficient** — wants comfort/reliability upgrades → focus on comfort, reliability, premium service, hassle-free ownership

## Brand Voice
Authentic, reliable, mission-driven. Every touchpoint reinforces core values and a customer-first mindset.

**Attributes:**
- Professional yet Accessible — simplify complex ideas for confident decisions
- Values-Driven — communicate with integrity and clear purpose
- Customer-First — service over selling, empathy and clarity

**Tagline:** "Solar That Works For You™"

Tone always stays optimistic, empowering, reassuring, and inspirational, but shifts by context:

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Social Media | Enthusiastic, Conversational |
| Customer Service | Supportive, Reassuring |
| Marketing & Products | Inspirational, Informative |
| Training & Education | Educational, Empowering |
| Internal Communications | Collaborative, Motivational |

## Messaging Guide
- Communicate trust, longevity, reliability — SolarTech is one of the longest-running solar companies; voice should feel confident, knowledgeable, steady, never reactive or exaggerated
- Speak as a trusted advisor focused on long-term value, not short-term hype
- Stay customer-centric: explain how SolarTech solves real energy problems through dependable systems, in-house service, and accountability before/during/after install
- Emphasize what differentiates SolarTech: long-term service and support after install (ties back to "Solar That Works For You™")
- Tone: professional, clear, approachable; urgency when appropriate but never pressure-driven
- No buzzwords or exaggerated claims — straightforward language backed by experience and proof
- Passion should show through commitment to quality, community, and sustainable growth — not flashy language
- Consistency matters: voice, structure, and intent should align across ads, social, website, and event materials

## Writing Rules
- Oxford comma in all lists
- Active voice
- Spell out numbers one through nine; use numerals for 10 and above
- No hype, exaggerated claims, or over-promising — all statements must be accurate, responsible, and aligned with customer expectations
- Short paragraphs: ideally 2–3 sentences
- Use bullet lists to organize multiple points for scannability

## CTA Standards

**Approved:**
- Get a Free Quote
- Schedule Your Consultation
- See If Your Home Qualifies
- Explore Solar Options
- Request Your System Audit

**Not approved:**
- "Hit us up"
- "Tap here for a crazy deal"
- "Click click click"
- "Don't miss out!"

## Ad Copy Rules
- Headlines: 4–8 words
- Structure: Feature → Benefit → CTA
- No hype terms
- Must align with "Solar That Works For You™"

## Social Media Rules
- Caption structure: **Hook → Value → CTA → Link** (every post includes a relevant link)
- Emojis: sparing, if used at all
- Hashtags: encouraged, more is fine for reach
- Balance imagery and text — avoid text-heavy visuals; keep the feed clean and visually approachable

## Non-Negotiables / Quality Checklist
Before shipping any SolarTech content, confirm:
- Does it sound like SolarTech? (voice/tone match)
- Does it look like SolarTech? (visual spec match)
- Does it follow formatting and tone rules?
- Is the CTA an approved one?

If any answer is "no," revise before publishing. If content doesn't educate, build trust, or strengthen the brand, it shouldn't ship.
